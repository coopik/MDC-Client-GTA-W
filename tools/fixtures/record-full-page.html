SOURCE OF ACTIVITY
On 26/SEP/2026, I, Police Officer I Raymond Peck (#222864), assigned to Mission Row Division, partnered with Police Officer III Scott Hinsley (#15623), assigned to Mission Row Division, were deployed under Unit 2A56. I was driving a marked black and white with a rooftop light bar. I was wearing my department-issued patrol uniform and was openly displaying my badge of office on my uniform. At approximately 15:30 hours, I was driving on Elgin Avenue when I got behind a black Buffalo STX, index "DER-649". My unit's ALPR alerted on the Buffalo's license plate that the vehicle's insurance has expired. I activated my unit's ELS and positioned myself behind the vehicle to initiate a traffic stop. The vehicle came to a full stop on the side of the road shortly after. After calling it in on the radio, I made contact with the driver and asked him for his license. I informed him that his insurance has expired and asked him to sort it out with his insurance company while I check his documents, but he denied the opportunity to resolve the situation on the scene. I told him, that he couldn't legally operate his vehicle until he renews his insurance. The driver's attitude became agressive, and he started cursing at me. I tried to de-escalate the situation, but the driver accelerated rapidly and fled from the stop, almost hitting a pedestrian in the process.
INVESTIGATION
I observed the suspect, later identified as John Doe, driving in an extremely reckless manner and at excessively high speeds (above 100mph) as he attempted to evade law enforcement. The pursuit lasted for approximately fifteen minutes, spanning throughout South Central and Downtown Los Santos. John Doe's vehicle eventually got spiked by another responding unit. After John Doe's vehicle came to a full stop, he exited the vehicle and fled on foot. A short foot pursuit ensued but I managed to get in John Doe's way and tackle him.

After tackling him, I, alongside three other officers, restrained him and brought him into custody. He was then searched in front of my unit's DICV by myself, where a Glock 19 was found. The aforementioned item was placed in an evidence bag on-scene, and later booked in at the MRS evidence locker.

After John Doe had been searched, he was placed in the back of our patrol unit where we then transported him to MRS. Due to John Doe's biometrics being on file, he was placed in a holding cell where he remained for a couple of hours. Early in the evening, SADCR arrived and he was then transported to a state correctional facility.
ARREST
I admonished John Doe utilizing my Field Officer’s Notebook, reading the following, verbatim:
“You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say may be used against you in a court of law. You have the right to the presence of an attorney during any questioning. If you cannot afford an attorney, one will be appointed to you, free of charge, before any questioning, if you want. Do you understand?”
John Doe responded affirmatively.
I transported John Doe to Mission Row Station.
John Doe was searched in front of a police vehicle, which was covered by the vehicle's Digital In-Car Video (DICV).
John Doe was arrested for FC 115. Evading a Peace Officer, MC 419. Reckless Driving, MC 116. Resisting Arrest, FC 711. Possession of a Firearm by Convicted Felon, IC 411. Excessive Speeding, IC 407. No Insurance.
